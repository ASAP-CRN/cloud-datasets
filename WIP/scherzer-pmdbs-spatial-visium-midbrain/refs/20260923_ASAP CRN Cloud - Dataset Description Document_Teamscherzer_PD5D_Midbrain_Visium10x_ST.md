# **Dataset Details**

Please limit the use of acronyms to well-established ones and avoid team-specific branding.

| Dataset Title | PD5D Midbrain Visium 10x Spatial Transcriptomics |
| ----: | :---- |
| **Dataset Description** | Visium 10x spatial transcriptomic raw data in FASTQ format of the midbrain of 100 human subjects. Individuals with incidental Lewy body disease, clinical PD, and healthy control were included. In addition to subject-level metadata, Visium spatial metadata such as H\&E images and spot coordinates are included. These data are one component of the PD5D cohort, which intends to build a multi-dimensional and multi-omic atlas of PD pathology. Therefore, other omics assays have been collected including genotyping, snRNA-seq, and scATAC-seq across two brain tissues: middle temporal gyrus and substantia nigra.  |

# **Project Information**

Provide the name and a brief description of the overall project to which this dataset belongs.  These should match the STUDY.csv table metadata entries.

| Project Name | Parkinson5D: deconstructing proximal disease mechanisms across cells, space, and progression |
| ----: | :---- |
| **Project Description** | Here we will develop a molecular atlas of Parkinson's disease (PD) useful for mapping GWAS/familial genetics to proximal mechanisms in five dimensions: brain cells (1D), brain space (3D), and disease stage (1D). We will reveal how genetic variants modulate transcription in specific cells in specific topographic locations of midbrain and cortex during the progression of neuropathology from healthy brains to prodromal to symptomatic disease. This research will for the first time integrate whole genome sequencing, single-nucleus transcriptomics, and high-resolution spatial transcriptomics of 100 human brains with cell-specific mechanistic analyses in model systems.  |
